# Level 3 difficulty pass — implementation and evidence

28 October 2026. Started on `lvl3-visual-refresh`, HEAD `e87e45d`. The two supplied difficulty documents were untracked initially and were subsequently staged externally; their contents/index state were preserved. No reset, stash, commit, push or publication was performed.

All four encounter changes are implemented. Large-panel counts remain six in the first two cars and eight elsewhere. Four Cargo pool members now form the authored pairs; the other four terminate overhead in the downstream exit, away from the authored lanes, gangway, landing and freight decision. Small instanced scraps remain harmless.

Cargo repeats on the registered scaled clock: **0–1.2 warning/right; 1.2–2.1 burst/right; 2.1–3.3 recovery; 3.3–4.5 warning/left; 4.5–5.4 burst/left; 5.4–8.4 clear**. Tilt direction, visible panels and a horizontal recovery arm convey phase without colour alone. Existing master/mute/pause-aware warning audio supplies the cue. Re-entry receives the current cue; the observation position remains safe throughout an existing cycle.

The `1.2 s` warning exceeds `1.7 / 4 + 0.25 + 0.1 = 0.775 s`: walking lateral travel, a reaction allowance and a 100 ms frame. The `1.2 s` recovery also exceeds a full `3.4 / 4 = 0.85 s` sideways lane change. Tests use complete rotated OBB sweeps, not a static gap calculation. Occupancy on the observation/landing/freight pads is checked across multiple cycles and seeds. Centre occupancy inside the selected burst zone is now lethal; other cars retain the previous centre/sheltered assertions.

The final movement beat fits the original **7 m** solid stretch from collapse lip `-3` to cab doorway `-10`; the fallback was unnecessary. The demonstrated route reaches solid floor near `-3.65`, crouches from about `-4.1` through `-5.1`, releases crouch separately, and launches the rail jump near `-5.9`. Landing occurs before the cab doorway. The beam's effective longitudinal body crossing is `0.18 + 2×0.18 = 0.54 m` (about `0.32 s` at `1.68 m/s` crouch speed), with a separate stand/run-up interval before the rail. Hold and toggle crouch use the real controller, including the toggle release edge.

Sprint `6.8`, wave `6.2`, lead `12`, depletion trigger, brake timing, original holes/supports, checkpoint positions and energy rules were preserved. Max energy is `100`; recharge `15/s`; Slow/Freeze/Rewind `18/28/32/s`; Ghost `35 + 1.5/s`; recovery floor `50`; L3 strain stays disabled. Vault, Convergence's crouched route, Mechanical's recess, Relay, Security decks/island, structural openings/windows, L2 and Ending were not retuned. New state uses existing snapshot/reset registration; continuous Rewind sweep history is retained, while cycle/reset discontinuities use the original collision reset rules. New signs retire with Cargo and dispose through the existing root lifecycle. No light, physics engine, post stack or dependency was added; the pool remains 12 lights.

The main-game keyboard run [Cargo through Ending](lvl3-difficulty/full/results.json) cleared both authored bursts, repaired the gangway, recharged on its landing, cleared door/load/freight, used Relay Ghost, traversed both Security holes, performed the Passenger weave/collapse/duck/jump and interacted with the actual brake. It used no pose changes, collision suppression or energy injection. Passenger finished with `85.5` energy; minimum wave gap was `12.06 m`. Cargo's repaired landing was reached with `52.7` energy, followed by an ordinary recharge and reuse of Freeze.

The [narrow performance-tier retry](lvl3-difficulty/passenger-retry-first-hold-performance-narrow-repeat/results.json) first spent energy legally before depletion, then blindly sprinted into the first seat and was caught by the wave. Actual respawn restored `50.50` energy at the depleted checkpoint. Pause held player position, energy and wave exactly. Deliberate play then reached the brake with `47.82` energy, minimum energy `12.82`, and minimum wave gap `9.93 m`; a second actual brake→Ending run also passed. Fixed/mixed controller tests from minimum recovery energy finish in roughly `4.5–4.7 s`, with minimum energy at least `11.36` and minimum wave gap at least `10.72 m`, including extra lateral input and three retries.

[Cargo retry evidence](lvl3-difficulty/cargo-retry/results.json) includes an actual unsupported fall, automatic checkpoint recovery, Slow→Freeze→normal, both dodges, gangway repair, freight, keyboard restart, and the real Quit to Title button (`level: null`, zero wreck roots). The continuous route also checked Ghost expiry on its pad, another summon and cancel after latching, and natural rear retirement. [Verified first-person hold crouch](lvl3-difficulty/passenger-first-hold-final/results.json) and [third-person toggle crouch](lvl3-difficulty/passenger-third-toggle/results.json) both reached Ending. Browser captures were visually inspected; this was an agent-driven browser input pass, not a human usability study.

The longer Cargo check used separate Chrome sessions in baseline/new/new/baseline order, each with 10 s warmup and 40 s sampling. It did not reproduce the isolated 66.7 ms p95 from one short new-source capture. Cargo's sustained median/p95 stayed comparable; GPU ranges overlap. Passenger's short median/p95 also stayed comparable, although that sample's CPU/GPU costs were higher. Static overhead captures were Cargo `33.3/49.9 → 33.2/50.0 ms` and Passenger `16.7/33.4 → 16.7/33.3 ms`. Resident counts are renderer observations at each capture endpoint, not total construction allocations; the longer captures have had more time for resource collection. These results do not establish improved FPS or a general absence of regressions. Available GPU pass samples, bloom/final/minimap costs and all raw frames are linked above. A longer Passenger profile and extended thermal/memory soak were not performed.

Profiling preceded the final Passenger-only prompt priority refinement: after the player clears the hole, the old protected floor hint now yields to the bound duck/jump instructions. Geometry, schedules, camera placement and Cargo's profiled execution path are unchanged by that refinement; it was checked again in controller tests and the actual first-person run. Captured source hashes and the delivered source audit are recorded alongside the measurements.

The [delivered-source audit](lvl3-difficulty/source-validation.json) records both captured and delivered hashes, including that final prompt-only difference.

Limits: fixed 30/60/144 FPS and mixed 100 ms deltas were exercised in the real-controller simulation; browser rendering ran at the hardware's available rate. Headless audio graph/reset behavior is covered, but no human listening check was performed. These captures and repeated level transitions are not an extended thermal/memory soak, and the scripted successful route does not establish novice success rates. No standalone new L2 visual playthrough or remote deployment was performed.
