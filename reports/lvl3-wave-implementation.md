# Level 3 pursuing time wave

The original finale field pursues the player throughout Level 3, starting **5.5 m behind the default Vault spawn, inside the carriage**. It moves at a **constant 3.6 m/s in the opening Vault**, **4.0 m/s through Convergence**, then **4.4 m/s from Mechanical onward**. Player movement, stopping and distance do not change its speed within a carriage. The final chase moves at **5.0 m/s**. On 28 October these speeds were reduced by about 20% to give the player more time; sprinting at 6.8 m/s now gains 1.8 m per second on the finale field, previously 0.6 m. Slow scales movement, Freeze stops it, and Rewind restores its recorded position. Spawn placement and appearance are unchanged.

The original two-sided shader, opacity, intensity and purple light are retained. The plane now faces the escape route: the player sees its purple scan bands instead of the back-facing normal's fully lit Fresnel wash. The field participates in the existing camera obstruction check, keeping the follow camera on the player's side instead of hiding one face or dimming the effect. Regression coverage checks initial placement, face orientation, camera clearance and Passenger/final pursuit speed.

Passenger retains the depletion trigger and Freeze-only abilities, with the final pursuit reduced to 5.0 m/s. It keeps the existing field moving without teleporting or restarting it. The depleted checkpoint retains its original 12 m retry lead. Pulling the brake stops pursuit. No new mesh, light, dependency or shared game system was added.

Latest validation: **5 focused chase tests pass**, exercising every carriage start, both opening-carriage speed transitions, equal speed while stopped/walking/farther ahead, time powers, retries and the reduced final-chase speed at fixed/mixed deltas. Production build and `git diff --check` pass. Existing material and large bundle warnings remain. The earlier 320-test full run and browser routes below used earlier pacing. Full race balance and the older tests' long stationary puzzle waits were not retuned or rerun for this speed reduction.
